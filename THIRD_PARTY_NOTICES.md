# Third-party material

The MIT and CC BY 4.0 grants cover the authors' original contributions.
They do not replace third-party license terms.

- `paper/acmart.cls`, `paper/ACM-Reference-Format.bst` and
  `paper/acmart-source/` contain ACM manuscript support files. Their original
  copyright and license notices are retained, together with class sources.
- The Python dependencies are installed separately. Their licenses remain
  those distributed with the corresponding packages; no virtual environment,
  wheel collection or dependency binaries are included.
- Physical-design measurements and extracted windows derive from the OpenROAD
  Flow Scripts Nangate45 benchmark flow. The RTL designs, standard-cell
  libraries, OpenROAD binaries, container image and complete design databases
  are not redistributed here. Obtain them from their upstream distributions
  under the upstream licenses. The recorded ORFS revision and container digest
  are listed in `docs/REPRODUCING.md`.

The public export does not grant new rights in third-party design names,
identifiers or material referenced by the benchmark records.
